How alerts work

On ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, healthcare-related text is checked after you pause typing. When a configured pattern is detected, an advisory alert appears on the page. Nothing is automatically blocked or changed.

No prompt history is stored by this extension. Do not enter identifiable patient information. Prototype only.
